Google Drive Images

Paste a Google Drive share link — it will be auto-converted to a working image URL.

1
Open Google Drive and find your image.
2
Right-click the image → Share → change access to "Anyone with the link"
3
Click Copy link — it looks like:
drive.google.com/file/d/FILE_ID/view
4
Paste it in the Paste Share Link tab → hit Convert → Add to Project.

💡 Tip: You can also paste Drive links directly in the URL field on the Media card — it auto-converts them too.

Media Storage
Connect ImgBB to upload images directly from the admin — no copy-pasting, no sharing settings.
ImgBB API Key

ImgBB is free image hosting with a simple API. Once connected, every image you drag into the Media zone uploads automatically and saves as a permanent URL.

1
Go to api.imgbb.com and sign up free
2
Click Get API Key — it's instant, no card needed
3
Paste it below and save
How it works
Before
Drag image → stored as base64 → fills localStorage → save fails ❌
After
Drag image → uploads to ImgBB → saves as URL → no storage limit ✅
